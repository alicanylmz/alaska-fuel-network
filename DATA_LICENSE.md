# Data licensing and attribution

The MIT License in `LICENSE` applies only to original code and documentation in
this repository. It does not replace the terms attached to source data.

## U.S. federal sources

The EIA, EPA, and USACE files are U.S. federal-government sources. U.S.
government works are generally not eligible for U.S. copyright protection
under 17 U.S.C. §105, but agency attribution, source disclaimers, third-party
content, and copyright outside the United States may still apply.

- U.S. Copyright Office: https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#105
- USAGov guidance: https://www.usa.gov/government-copyright

## HIFLD Petroleum Terminals

The surviving Data.gov metadata identifies the former HIFLD Petroleum
Terminals dataset as public access and links to the U.S. government-works
notice:

https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/petroleum-terminals

The exact archived snapshot and its accompanying metadata should be retained
together. The repository does not assert that every possible third-party field
incorporated by HIFLD is independently free of restrictions.

## Alaska state-government data

The federal-government-work rule does not automatically apply to Alaska state
or local government materials. ADEC and Alaska DCCED/DCRA materials retain the
terms stated by their publishers. Their inclusion here is for research
reproducibility and does not transfer ownership.
